# 🛒 SmartCart Customer Segmentation using Unsupervised Machine Learning

## 📌 Project Overview

In today's competitive e-commerce landscape, understanding customer behavior is critical for delivering personalized experiences and maximizing customer lifetime value. This project develops an intelligent **Customer Segmentation System** for SmartCart, a rapidly growing global e-commerce platform, using **Unsupervised Machine Learning** techniques.

Leveraging a dataset of **2,240 customer records** and **22 business attributes** spanning demographics, purchasing behavior, website engagement, and campaign responses, the solution identifies hidden customer groups with similar characteristics and buying patterns. The objective is to transform raw customer data into actionable business insights that enable targeted marketing, improved customer retention, and data-driven decision-making.

## 🚀 Key Highlights

* Performed comprehensive **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to uncover customer trends and behavioral patterns.
* Conducted extensive **data preprocessing**, including missing value treatment, feature transformation, scaling, and outlier analysis.
* Applied **Dimensionality Reduction Techniques** to improve cluster visualization and model performance.
* Implemented and compared multiple **Clustering Algorithms**, including K-Means clustering.
* Used clustering evaluation techniques such as the **Elbow Method** and **Silhouette Score** to determine the optimal number of customer segments.
* Created meaningful customer personas based on purchasing habits, engagement levels, and loyalty indicators.
* Developed actionable business recommendations for marketing and customer retention strategies.

## 🎯 Business Impact

The segmentation framework enables SmartCart to:

* Identify high-value customers for premium marketing campaigns.
* Detect churn-prone customers before disengagement occurs.
* Design personalized promotions tailored to specific customer segments.
* Improve customer retention and loyalty through targeted engagement.
* Optimize marketing spend by focusing on the most profitable customer groups.
* Support strategic decision-making through data-driven customer insights.
